# cryptobot-desk window

Scope: the whole desk window — rail, Live, History, Parameters, Runs, Log.
Visitor mode: Operate. One operator glancing at a window left open while the bot runs;
occasional deliberate review. Decisions in this brief were delegated by the operator to
the Jev decision model (direction A at 0.99; dark default at 0.83).

Audience and job: see if the bot is alive, whether anything is wrong, and whether
anything traded, in two seconds; then, on purpose, see where opportunities die, what
money moved, and whether the feed is healthy. Controls and their typed confirmations
stay exactly as strict as before.

Unresolved: Helius credit usage has no API here — shown as "not measurable from here",
never estimated as fact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The window is one moulded analyzer faceplate, not a grid of dashboard cards.
Every data stream is a coloured channel whose colour means the same thing on every
view. It refuses the category default: neon-on-black trading terminal, flat KPI tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Dark graphite clay ground; modules pressed up out of the faceplate with soft
paired shadows (cool highlight up-left, deep shadow down-right) and generous radii;
readings sit in recessed wells with inner shadow. Channels: amber = opportunity and
edge, teal = money and wallet, violet = feed and infrastructure, coral = fault only.
Light theme is pale bone clay with the same channels. System UI face (Segoe UI
Variable) for words; Cascadia Mono only for measured numerals.

STORY: The operator reads the four wells, sees the funnel narrow to zero and the one
reason it narrowed, and trusts that nothing coral means nothing broken.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rail left (power keys, mode switch, wallet well, nav). Top: four wells
across — bot state, edge against fee wall, money against the $1 budget, feed health.
Middle: the trade funnel as a row of chambers with drop reasons, beside the ranked
failure reasons. Below: divergence scope beside per-venue drift, then health and money.

FORM: Laboratory analyzer front panel, position 3 of 7 on the grounded list; seed key
94972659. Raised by teletext (declined): one meaning per colour and explanations behind a
REVEAL key. Raised by cracktro (declined): state as depth — stale readings sink into
their wells. Raised by tensegrity (declined): every figure drawn against its counterforce.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
