Every venue quoting one pair, drawn as distance in basis points from their own consensus. Absolute price hides this: a 2 bp gap on a $90 asset is invisible on a price axis. Where the lines separate is the entire business.

What a bigger book would have taken

The same opportunities, priced at each book size. Not extrapolated — measured at every rung on every episode.

Value against lifetime

Both axes logarithmic; horizontal is how long the opportunity survived, vertical is what it was worth. The two run in opposite directions, and that is the finding the whole project rests on.

Is the contest model measuring anything?

What refusing contested races costs

A contested cycle is charged a tip large enough to win the race and then refused for being contested — competition priced twice, once as a haircut and again as a refusal. A lost race costs the base fee or nothing at all, because the bundle does not land, so the downside of trying is close to zero. Which rung is real cannot be settled on paper; only trying settles it.

Trading parameters

Saving ends the current run. The ledger is archived and a new measurement begins. Rows recorded under different parameters aggregate by different rules, and nothing downstream would show the mixture — so the run is cut rather than quietly contaminated.

Wallet

The measured edge is currently negative. The instrument's own leaderboard shows a typical tradeable edge below the 2 bps it costs to cross two pools, and §4 of HANDOVER records an unexplained result that makes the headline profit unproven. A key here does not change that. Use a wallet holding only what you are prepared to lose.
